Slide 04 // The Clinical Case for IoT

The Clinical Paradigm Shift: Discrete vs Continuous Telemetry

Old Hospitals: Discrete Polling

A nurse visits every 4–6 hours to record vitals in a book. If a patient experiences acute myocardial ischemia between visits, physicians remain completely blind until the patient enters full cardiac arrest.

Modern Hospitals: Continuous IoT Streaming

Sensors stream SpO2, heart rate, and ECG to AWS IoT Core every 60 seconds. Real-time dashboards visualize subtle trends, predicting clinical deterioration (such as sepsis) 24–48 hours in advance.

Speaker Notes: In an old hospital, nurses measure vitals in discrete 4-hour intervals. With our cloud IoT streaming pipeline, telemetry streams continuously every minute, predicting complications before they occur.

Slide 06 // Data Migration

Low-Downtime Data Migration Architecture (AWS DMS & Glue)

AWS DMS Ongoing Replication

  • Multi-AZ DMS replication instance in private subnets.
  • Full load of historical records + continuous Change Data Capture (CDC).
  • Cutover window reduced to under 15 minutes during off-peak hours.

AWS Glue PySpark Integrity Engine

  • Cleanses and converts 5+ years of archive data into partitioned Parquet.
  • Applies HMAC-SHA256 pseudonymization for GDPR compliance.
  • Computes SHA-256 row-level checksums to verify zero data loss against source.
Speaker Notes: AWS DMS continuously captures live changes during testing with less than 2 seconds lag. AWS Glue processes archives into Parquet and validates cryptographic checksums for healthcare auditing.

Slide 11 // Compliance & Governance

Healthcare Regulatory Governance (GDPR, HIPAA, ISO 27001)

GDPR Article 17 (Right to Erasure)

Direct patient identifiers are pseudonymized with HMAC-SHA256 salted hashes. Deleting the patient's individual decryption key executes cryptographic erasure, instantly rendering records unreadable without breaking analytics integrity.

HIPAA Audit Integrity (WORM)

CloudTrail logs and medical audit records are sealed using Amazon S3 Object Lock in Compliance Mode (Write Once, Read Many). Even root administrators cannot alter or delete audit trails for 7 years.

Speaker Notes: We satisfy GDPR Article 17 through cryptographic pseudonymization and erasure. For HIPAA, audit logs are locked in S3 compliance mode so no one can modify them.
